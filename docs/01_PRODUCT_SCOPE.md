# 01 — IMBHOLA V3 product scope

## Product
A unified public website and role-based skills-development workspace for South African employers, SDF consultants, HR/training teams, employees, managers, signatories, auditors and Imbhola administrators.

## Public experience (M1)
Home, Platform, Solutions, How It Works, Skills Development Explained, About, Pricing (request a quote, no unapproved prices), Resources/FAQ, Contact, Demo Request, Login, Signup, Terms, Privacy and Accessibility. Navigation must work; contact capture requires a securely configured backend before activation. Avoid invented client endorsements and performance claims.

## Authenticated journeys (M2–M5)
Employer/SDF registration, verified organizational ownership, managed team invitations, multi-organization membership, workforce imports and frozen snapshots, versioned OFO mapping, training plans and actual training, employee acknowledgement, evidence vault, WSP/ATR preparation, consultations and approvals, audit-ready packs and manual external submission tracking.

## Reporting guardrail
First supported SETA is **UNKNOWN**. Build configurable adapters and explicit not-configured UI states. Production official exports must fail closed until authorized publication/template source, year, validation fixture suite, reviewer approvals and version hashes are recorded. No direct SETA portal submission in R1.

## Boundaries
No live payroll calculation/HR API promise in initial release, no LMS, no guaranteed funding outcomes, no real PII in samples. Maintain traceability to original BR-01 through BR-16 and FR-1.1 through FR-10.6 in the private source package. Sensitive original PDFs/DOCX should be reviewed before being published.
